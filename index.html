📌 Simple Past Tense (Kejadian Masa Lalu)

Rumus: Subject + Verb 2

Dipakai untuk kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lalu (yesterday, last week, 2 hours ago).

1. The manager _______ the financial audit report yesterday.

2. We _______ our regional partners in Kuala Lumpur last month.

3. They _______ the tax server maintenance during the weekend.

4. She _______ the client's invoice on Monday morning.

5. I _______ the updated journal entries two hours ago.

6. The company _______ its new operational policy last year.

7. Who _______ the presentation during Friday's meeting?

8. We _______ any critical errors in yesterday's filing.

9. He _______ his work laptop at the office last night.

10. The finance team _______ the Q2 budget on time.

📖 Soal Cerita / Case Study:

"Yesterday was a challenging day for the finance department. At 9 AM, our team (A. gathered / B. gathers) in the main room to discuss the annual tax audit. The auditor (C. points out / D. pointed out) three major discrepancies in our ledger. However, we (E. resolved / F. resolve) all issues before 5 PM."

📌 Simple Present Tense (Rutinitas & Fakta)

Rumus: Subject + Verb 1 (+s/es untuk He/She/It)

Dipakai untuk rutinitas kerja harian, regulasi, atau jadwal tetap.

1. Our department _______ weekly progress reports every Monday.

2. Senior accountants _______ complex tax reconciliation tasks.

3. The HR team always _______ new employees on their first day.

4. She _______ work at 8 AM every morning without fail.

5. We _______ third-party software for payroll management.

6. The tax portal _______ maintenance every Sunday midnight.

7. My supervisor usually _______ emails within one hour.

8. Small businesses _______ clear inventory tracking to calculate COGS.

9. He rarely _______ lunch at his desk.

10. Local regulations _______ companies to keep records for 5 years.

📖 Soal Cerita / Case Study:

"Sarah is a finance executive. Every morning, she (A. check / B. checks) all outstanding invoices. Her colleague, David, (C. prepare / D. prepares) the daily cash flow summary. Together, they (E. ensure / F. ensures) that all vendor payments are scheduled accurately."

📌 Present Continuous Tense (Sedang Berlangsung)

Rumus: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

Dipakai untuk aktivitas yang sedang terjadi saat ini atau proyek yang sedang berjalan saat ini.

1. Right now, I _______ the regional financial report.

2. The IT team _______ the corporate server this morning.

3. Please speak quietly; they _______ a confidential call.

4. She _______ with the tax office regarding our WHT rates.

5. We _______ a new inventory management web app this month.

6. Look! The auditor _______ through our physical receipts.

7. Why _______ you _______ late today?

8. Currently, the director _______ the budget proposal.

9. They _______ the new salary structure this week.

10. I _______ for the bus to head to the office right now.

📖 Soal Cerita / Case Study:

"If you visit our office right now, you will see a busy scene. The design team (A. is pitching / B. pitches) their new branding concept. Meanwhile, the accounting managers (C. are discussing / D. discuss) tax compliance updates, and I (E. am writing / F. write) a summary report for our board."

📌 Present Perfect Tense (Sudah/Belum Terjadi)

Rumus: Subject + have/has + Verb 3

Dipakai untuk pekerjaan yang sudah selesai dilakukan dan hasilnya relevan saat ini.

1. We _______ the quarterly financial statement.

2. She _______ working on the audit report.

3. I _______ the tax declaration form yet.

4. The CEO _______ the new budget allocation.

5. They _______ in Kuala Lumpur for three days now.

6. _______ you _______ the client payment receipt?

7. The vendor _______ their updated bank details.

8. We _______ this accounting software for over two years.

9. He _______ three client calls this morning.

10. The company _______ its annual target ahead of time.

📖 Soal Cerita / Case Study:

"Our team is ready for the end-of-year audit. We (A. have reconciled / B. reconciled) all accounts, and the lead auditor (C. has checked / D. checked) our inventory records. So far, no major issues (E. have emerged / F. emerged)."
