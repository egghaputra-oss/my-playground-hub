Modul Latihan Tenses Esensial untuk Komunikasi Kerja

📌 Simple Past Tense (Kejadian Masa Lalu)

Rumus: Subject + Verb 2

Dipakai untuk kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lalu (yesterday, last week, 2 hours ago).

1. The manager _______ the financial audit report yesterday.

2. We _______ our regional partners in Kuala Lumpur last month.

3. She _______ the client's invoice on Monday morning.

"Yesterday, our team (A. gathered / B. gathers) to discuss the tax audit. The auditor (C. pointed out / D. points out) three main errors, but we (E. resolved / F. resolve) them before 5 PM."

📌 Simple Present Tense (Rutinitas & Fakta)

Rumus: Subject + Verb 1 (+s/es untuk He/She/It)

Dipakai untuk rutinitas kerja harian, regulasi, atau jadwal tetap.

1. Our department _______ weekly progress reports every Monday.

2. Senior accountants _______ complex tax reconciliation tasks.

📌 Present Continuous Tense (Sedang Berlangsung)

Rumus: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

Dipakai untuk aktivitas yang sedang terjadi saat ini.

1. Right now, I _______ the regional financial report.

📌 Present Perfect Tense (Sudah Selesai / Pengalaman)

Rumus: Subject + have/has + Verb 3

Dipakai untuk pekerjaan yang baru selesai dan hasilnya relevan sekarang.

1. We _______ the quarterly financial statement.
