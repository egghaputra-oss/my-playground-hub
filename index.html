📌 Simple Past Tense (Kejadian Masa Lalu)

Rumus: Subject + Verb 2

1. The manager _______ the financial audit report yesterday.

2. We _______ our regional partners in Kuala Lumpur last month.

📌 Simple Present Tense (Rutinitas & Fakta)

Rumus: Subject + Verb 1 (+s/es untuk He/She/It)

1. Our department _______ weekly progress reports every Monday.

📌 Present Continuous Tense (Sedang Berlangsung)

Rumus: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

1. Right now, I _______ the regional financial report.

📌 Present Perfect Tense (Sudah Selesai / Pengalaman)

Rumus: Subject + have/has + Verb 3

1. We _______ the quarterly financial statement.

💬 1. Asking & Giving Updates (5 Phrases)

1. "Where do we stand on [project]?"
2. "We are currently on track to meet the deadline."
3. "We ran into a minor bottleneck with..."
4. "I'll keep you posted as soon as I get an update."
5. "Could you give us a high-level overview?"

🛡️ 2. Interrupting & Disagreeing Politely (5 Phrases)

1. "Sorry to interrupt, but may I add a quick point?"
2. "I see your point, but from a cost perspective..."
3. "I respectfully have a slightly different view."
4. "Before we move on, can I clarify one detail?"
5. "I agree in principle, but we need to consider..."

⏳ 3. Buying Time & Clarifying (5 Phrases)

1. "That's a good question. Let me double-check..."
2. "Just to clarify, do you mean...?"
3. "If I understand correctly, you want us to..."
4. "I'm a bit tied up right now. Can I get back to you?"
5. "Could you walk me through that step again?"
