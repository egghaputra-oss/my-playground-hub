👋 1. Greeting & Casual Check-ins

1. "How's it going with you, Rebeca?"
2. "What have you been up to, Eggha?"
3. "How was your weekend, Adam?"
4. "Long time no see, Anita!"
5. "How are things with Zoey?"

☕ 2. Making Plans & Inviting Friends

1. "Do you want to grab a coffee, Eggha?"
2. "Are you free this Saturday, Rebeca?"
3. "Let's hang out at the new cafe!"
4. "What are you up to tonight, Zoey?"
5. "Sounds like a plan, Adam!"

💬 3. Expressing Opinions & Reacting

1. "To be honest, I really like it!"
2. "That's awesome, Anita!"
3. "I know what you mean, Zoey."
4. "No way, really?"
5. "Let me think about it, Rebeca."
